What goes with more casualties

Comparing London's small areas with a statistical model that allows for how busy each one is. Each bar shows the change in casualties per km of road for an area one standard deviation higher on that feature. Grey: not statistically significant. These are associations, not causes.

About this map

Police-recorded injury collisions (Department for Transport, STATS19) involving pedestrians in Greater London, linked to small areas, OpenStreetMap streets and features, deprivation and population.

Most dangerous street stretches use a kernel density along the street network (250 m), so clusters stay on the street where they happened. Hot and cold spots use Getis-Ord Gi* with 999 permutations. More than expected compares each area with a negative binomial model, corrected for multiple testing. Drive times assume free-flowing traffic.

Minor pedestrian injuries are under-reported to the police, and associations between areas and casualties do not show cause.
